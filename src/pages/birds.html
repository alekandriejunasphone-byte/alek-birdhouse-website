Native Birds

The Willie Wagtail

I researched the Willie Wagtail becuase its moving tail makes it easy to notice. It has a black head and back, a white belly and a white eyebrow.

It lives in places such as open woodland, parks and gardens. It eats insects that it catches on the ground or in the air.

Willie Wagtails build open cup-shaped nests from grass and spider web. My enclosed birdhouse is not an ideal nesting box for them.

Why birds need protecting

Birds need places to feed, shelter and nest. Looking after trees and keeping rubbish out of the enviroment are simple ways I can help.

I can watch nests from a distance and keep pets away from them. A real bird shelter should match the species, rather than just look nice.

Birds need diffrent homes

Eastern Rosellas use tree hollows for nesting. That is different from a Willie Wagtail’s cup nest, and it shows why I need to research the bird before choosing a box design.

Research: Australian Museum, BirdLife Australia and Canberra Birds.
