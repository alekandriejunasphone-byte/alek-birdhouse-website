My Birdhouse

I chose a sloping roof and porch. Bark looked natural.

Seven timber pieces, plus the plywood base. Check thickness, entrance size, roof angle and cutting layout with the teacher.

Parts of my design

  • Entrance: sized for the bird.
  • Porch and perch: class features; many nest boxes need no perch.
  • Angled roof: helps rain run off.
  • Bark: looks natural, but is not waterproofing.

Before outdoor use, check the bird’s needs, drainage, ventilation, cleaning access and placement.

My design ideas

I compared plain wood, bark-and-twig and planter ideas. Bark seemed simplest.
