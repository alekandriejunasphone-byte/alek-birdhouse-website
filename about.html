About My Project

My name is Alek Andriejunas. This was my Year 8 Technology Mandatory birdhouse project for 2026.

What I learnt

I learnt about measuring timber, joining pieces and using diffrent workshop tools safely. The project also helped me understand why birds need different types of nests.

The tricky parts

Getting the pieces to fit together is the part that needs the most care. I need to check the measurments before cutting and check the fit before nailing.

What I would improve

Next time I would spend more time checking the roof fit before attaching it. I would also research a particular bird’s needs before making a box to use outside, becuase the class design will not suit every bird.

I would add my own finished photos, sketches and workshop pictures to show the work properly. The reference pictures on this website are not photos of my build.

Feedback

Feedback about the project can go through my teacher or the school’s normal classroom channel.
